# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Football DFS players aged 21-35, primarily Indian market. Data-comfortable: they check xG after matches and use PrizePicks regularly. Mobile-first — checking picks on their phone at half-time or in transit. Secondary audience: World Cup 2026 casual fans discovering DFS.

## Product Purpose

AI-powered football prop pick recommendations built on real FBref stats. Gives DFS players a data-driven analytical edge with confidence scores, full AI reasoning, and matchup context. Stat categories: Shots on Target, Total Shots, Passes Completed, Fouls Committed, Corners Won.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Confident. Data-native. Bloomberg meets football — an analyst's tool made accessible. Not a tipster, not a sports broadcast: a quant in your pocket.

## Anti-references

- Generic green-accented sports betting apps (bet365, DraftKings default aesthetic)
- "AI startup" purple gradient SaaS landing pages
- Sports broadcast aesthetics (bold team-color stripes, animated scoreboards)
- Fantasy sports gamification (coins, level-up effects, confetti on wins)
- Warm cream / sand / paper backgrounds — this is a night-mode terminal product

## Design Principles

1. **Data is the hero** — picks and numbers come first, chrome comes last
2. **Precision over hype** — every word and every visual element earns its place; no decorative filler
3. **Terminal-native** — feels like a financial data terminal that became beautiful, not a sports app with dark mode bolted on
4. **Show, don't describe** — the product is demonstrated in the viewport; it is never merely described
5. **Mobile-first density** — information hierarchy that works at 375px first, scales up gracefully

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Color is never the sole indicator of meaning (MORE/LESS also uses text + position). prefers-reduced-motion respected on all animations. Touch targets 44px minimum.
